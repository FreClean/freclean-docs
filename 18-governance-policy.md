# 18 - Governance Policy

## Structure

| Role | Composition | Term |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | Johnny Dubic | Ongoing |
| Management group | Five members, identities private | Renewable five-year cycle |

## Founder role

Sets overall company direction, holds founder-level responsibilities as defined in this policy, and participates in treasury and governance decisions alongside the management group - not unilaterally for significant matters (see `17-treasury-policy.md`).

## Management group

- Five members, each serving a renewable five-year term.
- Appointment, renewal, rotation, and replacement procedures are to be finalized and documented here once confirmed.
- Voting and quorum rules are to be finalized alongside the group's operational rollout.
- Member identities are intentionally not published, in any FreClean document or repository, to protect individual privacy and security.

## Responsibilities

| Area | Responsible party |
|---|---|
| Company direction | Founder, with management group input |
| Treasury approval (above threshold) | Multi-signature, management group |
| Day-to-day operations | Operations role (see `freclean-api`'s RBAC roles) |
| Financial oversight | Finance role, management group |
| Emergency decisions | Founder + management group, procedure to be finalized |

## Conflict of interest

A formal conflict-of-interest policy is to be drafted and adopted as part of finalizing this governance structure - not yet published.

## Internal controls & record-keeping

- All treasury transactions are recorded - see `freclean-admin`'s Audit Logs.
- Governance decisions of record (appointments, policy changes) will be logged once the recording mechanism is finalized.

## Governance review

This policy is reviewed on a cadence to be defined once the management group is operationally active - not less often than each five-year term renewal cycle.
