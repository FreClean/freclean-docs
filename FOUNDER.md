Founder

Johnny Dubic

Founder of CeloHT and FreClean

Johnny Dubic is a Haitian entrepreneur and builder whose work brings together technology, financial inclusion, entrepreneurship, education, and practical community development.

He is the founder of CeloHT and FreClean, two initiatives that operate in different fields but share a common belief that meaningful economic opportunity begins with practical solutions that people can understand, access, and use.

His work started in Léogâne, Haiti, and has grown around a broader ambition to build Haitian-founded initiatives with relevance beyond Haiti.

CeloHT

Johnny founded CeloHT in 2022 in Léogâne, Haiti.

CeloHT was created around a straightforward question.

How can blockchain technology become useful to people who are not developers, traders, or technology specialists?

The answer became an initiative centered on education, financial inclusion, community access, and practical use of digital financial infrastructure.

CeloHT developed around three principal areas of work.

Education

Helping people understand blockchain, digital finance, wallets, financial literacy, and the practical use of emerging technologies.

Agents

Building a bridge between digital financial infrastructure and people in local communities through trusted individuals who can help users understand and access digital financial services.

Reforestation

Connecting economic and technological development with environmental responsibility through community-oriented environmental initiatives.

CeloHT is built as an open-source initiative within the Celo ecosystem. Its public repositories and documentation are intended to make the work visible, understandable, and open to participation.

The Idea Behind CeloHT

For Johnny, financial inclusion is not simply about giving someone access to an application.

A person needs to understand the tool.

They need to trust it.

They need to know how to use it safely.

And, most importantly, the technology needs to have a reason to exist in their everyday life.

This thinking shaped CeloHT's education-first approach.

Instead of beginning with technology and searching for a problem to solve, the project starts with people and the problems they already face.

That is why education, community access, digital payments, entrepreneurship, and environmental impact became connected parts of the CeloHT vision.

FreClean

Johnny is also the founder of FreClean, an independent business focused on professional cleaning services and cleaning products.

FreClean represents another side of his entrepreneurial work.

Where CeloHT explores financial technology and digital infrastructure, FreClean operates in the physical economy through services, products, employment opportunities, and entrepreneurship.

The company focuses on professional cleaning and the development and distribution of cleaning products, with an emphasis on quality, sustainability, innovation, and economic opportunity.

For Johnny, entrepreneurship does not have to remain theoretical.

A business should solve a real problem.

It should create value for customers.

It should create opportunities for people who work with it.

And it should have the ability to grow beyond its original market.

Where CeloHT and FreClean Meet

CeloHT and FreClean are separate initiatives.

They have different business models, different operational purposes, and different areas of activity.

What connects them is the founder's broader approach to economic participation.

FreClean operates in the real economy.

CeloHT works on digital financial infrastructure, education, and community access.

Together, they provide an example of how a traditional service business can exist alongside a technology initiative without either one losing its identity.

CeloHT has publicly described collaboration with FreClean around digital payments, financial education, entrepreneurship, and local economic opportunity.

The connection is therefore practical.

Technology should support real economic activity.

Businesses should be able to benefit from better financial tools.

And people should be able to move gradually from traditional economic activity into the digital economy without having to abandon the systems they already understand.

Building From Haiti

Johnny's work is deeply connected to Haiti.

CeloHT was founded in Léogâne.

FreClean was also established in Léogâne.

Both initiatives began from a local context rather than from a distant international market.

That origin matters.

The objective is not simply to bring foreign ideas into Haiti.

It is to build from Haiti.

That means understanding local realities, experimenting with practical solutions, learning from the community, and developing models that can eventually be useful elsewhere.

CeloHT's public materials describe this as a broader effort to connect Haitian communities with the global digital economy.

A Builder Rather Than Only a Founder

Johnny's role goes beyond holding the title of founder.

Building an organization requires creating the things that make the organization function.

That includes documentation.

Education.

Community relationships.

Partnerships.

Technology.

Business development.

Public communication.

Research.

And the willingness to continue working when the original idea is still far from finished.

This is particularly important for CeloHT because the project is intentionally being developed in public.

The repositories, documentation, research, and project materials form part of the organization's public record.

Open Source and Transparency

Open source is central to CeloHT's identity.

Johnny's approach is that trust should be earned through visible work.

People should be able to examine the project.

They should be able to understand its direction.

They should be able to identify weaknesses.

They should be able to contribute.

And they should be able to disagree.

This philosophy is reflected in CeloHT's public development model and documentation.

The founder's responsibility is therefore not simply to control the project.

It is to build a foundation strong enough for other people to participate in it.

Education as Infrastructure

One of the strongest themes across Johnny's work is education.

CeloHT treats education as more than an additional program.

It is part of the infrastructure required for adoption.

A wallet without understanding can become a source of risk.

A digital payment system without financial literacy can remain inaccessible.

Blockchain technology without local education can remain limited to a small technical community.

CeloHT was designed to address that gap.

The goal is to make complicated ideas understandable enough that ordinary people can make informed decisions about the technologies available to them.

Entrepreneurship

FreClean reflects another important part of Johnny's philosophy.

Economic empowerment cannot depend exclusively on technology.

People also need businesses, customers, jobs, services, products, and opportunities to generate income.

That is why entrepreneurship is an important connection between Johnny's two ventures.

CeloHT explores how digital infrastructure can support economic participation.

FreClean creates an example of that participation in a practical business environment.

The two initiatives therefore approach opportunity from different directions.

One builds around technology.

The other builds around a service business.

Both begin with people.

Environmental Responsibility

Johnny's work also includes an environmental dimension.

CeloHT's reforestation pillar reflects the idea that development should not be measured only through financial or technological growth.

Communities also need a healthier environment.

Economic opportunity and environmental responsibility do not have to compete with each other.

They can become part of the same long-term development model.

This principle is incorporated into CeloHT's three-pillar structure.

Leadership

Johnny's leadership philosophy is built around responsibility rather than personality.

The founder sets direction.

The community contributes.

Builders create.

Partners collaborate.

Users provide feedback.

And the project evolves through the work of everyone involved.

For CeloHT, this means the founder is part of the foundation, but the future of the project should not depend entirely on one individual.

A successful open-source initiative must eventually become larger than its founder.

Looking Beyond the First Chapter

Johnny's work is still developing.

CeloHT is evolving its technology, documentation, community infrastructure, educational programs, and open-source ecosystem.

FreClean is developing its own path in cleaning services, products, and entrepreneurship.

Neither story is finished.

The objective is not to present either organization as something it has not yet become.

The objective is to continue building until the work speaks for itself.

From Léogâne to the wider Caribbean and the global digital economy, Johnny's long-term ambition is to demonstrate that Haitian founders can create organizations that are locally grounded while remaining globally relevant.

Founder Philosophy

At the center of Johnny's work is a simple principle.

Build something useful.

Make it understandable.

Make it accessible.

Be honest about what works and what does not.

Create opportunities for other people.

Keep learning.

And build institutions that can continue growing long after the first idea was created.
