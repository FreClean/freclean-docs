# 00 - Executive Summary

**FreClean** is a Haitian company built around a simple premise: a cleaning business that people actually use every week is one of the strongest possible foundations for real-world digital payment adoption - stronger than a purely speculative crypto product, because the demand for the underlying service (a clean home, a clean Airbnb, a clean office) does not depend on market sentiment.

FreClean operates across four connected pillars:

| Pillar | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Cleaning services | Airbnb, residential, office, hotel, and kitchen cleaning | Active |
| Cleaning & fragrance products | Formulated and/or resold cleaning products | In development |
| Entrepreneurship | A program for individuals to resell FreClean products | Planned |
| Web3 payments | Cash, card, and USDm payments via CeloHT | In development |

FreClean was founded by **Johnny Dubic** on **April 16, 2025**, and operates out of **Léogâne, Ouest, Haiti**.

## Why this document exists

This whitepaper is FreClean's single reference for its business model, technical architecture, Web3 strategy, governance, and roadmap. It is written to one standard throughout: **every claim is either true today, or explicitly labeled as planned, in development, or demo.** Nothing here is inflated to look more advanced than it is - see `24-legal-policy-framework.md` and the realism principle referenced in every section.

## What makes FreClean's Web3 approach different

Most consumer Web3 projects start with a token and look for a use case afterward. FreClean starts with a use case - a recurring, physical, cash-generating service business - and adds a Web3 payment rail (Celo, via CeloHT) as *one more way to pay*, alongside cash and card, not as the product itself. FreClean does not issue a token and does not operate its own blockchain. This is a deliberate positioning choice: **FreClean's credibility as a Web3 project should come from real transaction volume on real services, not from token speculation** - see `01-vision-and-positioning.md` for the full argument and the roadmap that would need to be executed to make that case at scale.
