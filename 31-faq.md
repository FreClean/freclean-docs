# 31 — FAQ

**Does FreClean have its own cryptocurrency or blockchain?**
No. FreClean accepts payments on the existing Celo network through its partner CeloHT. FreClean does not issue a token and does not operate its own blockchain — see `07-web3-strategy.md`.

**Is Web3 payment required to use FreClean?**
No. Cash and card payments are supported alongside Web3, and Web3 is never the only option.

**What happens if I send FreClean crypto and something goes wrong?**
Every Web3 payment is tracked through a five-stage status (`08-celo-integration.md`) and never marked confirmed until independently verified on Celo. If a payment doesn't complete, it moves to `failed` or `expired` rather than being left ambiguous.

**Does FreClean guarantee income through the Entrepreneur Program?**
No — explicitly, repeatedly, throughout `freclean-entrepreneurship`. It provides training, product access, and support; results depend on the entrepreneur's own effort.

**Is FreClean's product line certified or independently verified?**
Not yet. Products are marked with an honest lifecycle status (development, prototype, available, discontinued, planned) and no product is marketed as available before it verifiably is — see `04-product-strategy.md`.

**Who governs FreClean?**
A founder (Johnny Dubic) and a five-member management group operating on a renewable five-year cycle — see `18-governance-policy.md`. Management group member identities are kept private.

**Where can I see FreClean's real, current status?**
In this whitepaper and its companion repositories — every claim here is either true today or explicitly labeled otherwise (in development, planned, or demo). See `24-legal-policy-framework.md`.
