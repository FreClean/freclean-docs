# 24 - Legal / Policy Framework

## The realism principle

This is the single rule every document, repository, and interface across the FreClean ecosystem follows: **a status is stated as current, in development, planned, or demo - and never presented as more advanced than it actually is.** Concretely:

- No business registration number, tax ID, license, certification, revenue figure, customer count, employee count, or partnership is invented anywhere in this ecosystem.
- No product is marked available before it verifiably is (enforced by CI in `freclean-products`).
- No blockchain transaction is fabricated; demo transactions are explicitly labeled `DEMO`.
- No feature is described as live when it is only planned.

## Regulatory posture on Web3

FreClean has not sought or received any regulatory opinion, license, or approval specific to its Web3 payment functionality. Web3 payments are offered as an optional payment rail, not an investment product, security, or financial instrument - see the explicit exclusions in `07-web3-strategy.md`. This document does not constitute legal advice on the regulatory status of stablecoin payments in Haiti or any other jurisdiction.
