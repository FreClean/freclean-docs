# 27 - Sustainability

## Environmental

- Packaging material choice (recyclability, refill programs) is not yet finalized - see `freclean-products/docs/PACKAGING-GUIDELINES.md`.
- Product formulation aims to balance effectiveness with environmental impact; specific environmental certifications are not claimed until earned and verified.

## Economic

- The Entrepreneurship Program (`06-entrepreneurship-model.md`) is designed to distribute economic opportunity locally rather than concentrate it, consistent with FreClean's roots in Léogâne.
- Local-first sourcing (`04-product-strategy.md`) keeps more of the supply chain within Haiti where feasible.

## Operational

- Checklist-driven operations (`freclean-operations`) reduce waste from inconsistent product use.
- Web3 payments, where adopted, reduce dependency on cash handling and traditional banking infrastructure that may be less accessible in FreClean's operating area.

## What is not yet claimed

FreClean does not currently claim any formal sustainability certification (e.g. B Corp, ISO 14001) or publish carbon accounting - these would only be claimed once actually pursued and verified.
