# 22 — Product Safety (Summary)

Full detail: `freclean-products/docs/SDS-TEMPLATE.md` and `freclean-products/docs/PRODUCT-LIFECYCLE.md`.

## Rule

No product reaches `available` status without a completed Safety Data Sheet and a real SKU — enforced by CI validation (`freclean-products/scripts/validate-catalog.ts`), not left to manual review alone.

## Scope of a FreClean SDS

Identification, hazard classification, composition, first-aid measures, handling/storage, exposure controls, physical/chemical properties, stability, toxicological and ecological information, disposal, transport, and regulatory information — the full 16-section template used by every product.
